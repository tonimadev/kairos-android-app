Turn your smartphone and smartwatch into the best allies for your virtual meetings. Kairós is the ultimate Google Meet companion, so you never miss a call again!

No more waiting for meeting notifications or losing track of time while grabbing a coffee. Available for Android and Wear OS, Kairós syncs with your calendar and turns every important event into a full-screen alarm you can't ignore, whether you're at your desk or away from your keyboard. Add the Kairós complication to your watch face and see your next meeting at a glance.

Stay ahead of your commitments and keep your punctuality with productivity-focused features:

The ultimate meeting alert (phone & Wear OS): When your meeting time arrives, Kairós wakes up your phone with a full-screen alarm. On Wear OS the alert is shown prominently, so you know about your next Google Meet call even if your phone is locked or in your pocket.

Quick access to calls: Open the event in your calendar with a single tap on the alarm and reach your Google Meet link instantly.

Complications for Wear OS: Your schedule on your wrist. Add the Kairós complication (short text, long text or ranged value) to your watch face to see the title, the time or a countdown to your next meeting at a glance.

Exclusive Wear OS tile: Swipe on your watch to see your upcoming meetings and manage global alarms. You're up to date on your whole day in a single gesture.

Native integration: Works seamlessly with the calendars already set up on your Android, making it the ideal partner for anyone who uses Google Calendar every day.

Smart alarm filter: Total control. Turn alarms on or off for all events, or choose individually which meetings are crucial and deserve their own alarm, on your phone and on your watch.

Focused view: Move easily through your daily meetings and appointments with a clean calendar interface, and quickly see which days are the busiest.

Battery efficiency: With an intelligent background scheduling system, Kairós keeps you on time for meetings without draining your phone or smartwatch battery.
